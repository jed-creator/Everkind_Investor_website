Mission

To make wellbeing support something a person can reach for on any ordinary day. Private, affordable, and always available.

Support that requires an appointment, a referral, or a crisis to justify it goes unused on the ordinary days, and most days are ordinary. The result is a gap between the support people are offered and the support they actually reach for.

Everkind puts a private space in a person's pocket: a journal that listens, a voice that reflects, and meditations generated from their own words. It is designed to be reached for on an ordinary day, in whichever way is easiest that day.

Everkind supports wellbeing. It does not provide therapy, treatment, or clinical care, and it is not equipped for crisis situations. It sits alongside professional care. It never replaces it.
