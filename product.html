Product

Journaling, reflection, and meditation — in one quiet place.

Everkind adapts to how you actually want to show up that day: typed, spoken, or listening.

AI Journal

Write an entry and get a reflection back.

Everkind may notice a pattern you keep returning to, and offers a question to sit with.

Voice Mode

Some days writing is too much.

Speak instead, and Everkind reflects back in real time.

AI Meditation

A meditation generated from your own entry.

Your words, your day — not a stock library track.

Guest meditations.

Pre-recorded meditations recorded with clinicians who advise Everkind, including Intentional Breathing with David Drapkin, LCSW. These are recordings — no clinician is present, and this is not therapy.

Lock your journal.

Set a PIN so your entries stay yours. Write freely, knowing nobody else can open it.

An always-available wellbeing layer for your whole team.

Year-round, not a handful of sessions.

A typical workplace plan covers a small number of counselling sessions a year. Everkind is available every day of the year — as an alternative where there's no plan, or a supplement where there is.

Designed to be used.

The hardest part of workplace wellbeing isn't the offering — it's getting people to reach for it. Everkind is built to be opened on an ordinary day.

Private by design.

Leaders see anonymized, aggregate engagement — never an individual.

Quick to roll out.

Most teams are live within about a day. People opt in via the app.
