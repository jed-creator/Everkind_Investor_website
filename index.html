Everkind

A private space to feel understood, every day.

Everkind is an AI-powered wellbeing platform: journaling, reflection, and guided meditation, shaped with input from clinicians. Yours alone, whenever you reach for it.

Most people don't need a program. They need somewhere to put the day.

01

Write it down, or say it out loud.

Type an entry or speak it. Everkind meets you in whichever way is easier that day.

02

Get something back.

A reflection that may notice a pattern you keep returning to. Not advice, not a diagnosis. A question worth sitting with.

03

Then let it settle.

A meditation generated from what you wrote, in your words, about your day.

Your journal is yours.

Entries are private to the person who writes them. You can lock your journal behind a PIN.
